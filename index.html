Original Character

Keicyla Icelin

Keicyla Icelin is the original character created by Keyla. Before stepping into content creation and streaming, Keyla originally debuted as a VTuber. From her early VTubing days to her current avatar as a streamer, her characters have always featured a signature blue design, a reflection of her love for the color blue.

Keicyla story is shrouded in nighttime mystery. She is a creature of the dark who comes alive when the sun sets. She sustains herself by feeding on human souls while they sleep. But her touch is not purely terrifying in exchange for a soul, she leaves behind dreams so blissful and intoxicating that her victims are left craving more.
